# Editorial boundaries

## Faithful

Use when the user asks for translation or correction. Fix accuracy, grammar, terminology, and unnatural syntax. Preserve paragraph order, argument structure, examples, and the author's level of certainty.

## CT-native

Use when the user wants English that feels at home on Crypto Twitter or in an English crypto publication. Adapt established phrases and sentence rhythm, but do not introduce new jokes, stronger claims, or a different persona.

## Editorial

Use only with clear authorization to rewrite. You may restructure, cut repetition, strengthen transitions, or change pacing. Flag any change that materially alters emphasis or reasoning.

When uncertain, choose Faithful. A translation request alone is not permission to substantially rewrite the article.
