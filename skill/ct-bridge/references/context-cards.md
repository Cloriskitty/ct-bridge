# Context Cards

Use a context card when literal translation would leave the user missing the joke, stance, or social meaning.

## Compact form

```text
一句话意思
...

语气与潜台词
...

背景
...

需要注意
...
```

Omit sections that add no value. Do not force every response into the full template.

## Confidence

- **High**: the phrase has a stable meaning and the surrounding context supports it.
- **Medium**: the phrase is established but the author's intent could vary.
- **Low**: the interpretation depends on missing thread, image, event, or private community context.

## Example

Source: `ETH is cooked. Still, probably nothing.`

- `cooked` means in serious trouble or perceived as finished, not physically cooked.
- `probably nothing` is often ironic and can imply that something deserves attention.
- Together they may be mockery, engagement bait, or an intentionally contradictory meme. They do not prove the author's actual position.
