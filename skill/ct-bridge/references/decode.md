# Decode English Crypto Content

## Goal

Help a Chinese reader understand what the content means inside its actual crypto context. Translation alone is insufficient when the meaning depends on sarcasm, memes, author position, prior events, or community norms.

## Default response

Use the smallest useful context card:

1. **一句话意思**: direct Chinese explanation.
2. **语气与潜台词**: sincere, ironic, mocking, promotional, defensive, engagement-seeking, or uncertain.
3. **背景**: only the cultural, historical, or technical context necessary to understand it.
4. **需要注意**: ambiguity, missing thread context, or unsupported inference.

Do not explain basic crypto concepts to an expert unless they ask. When the user says they know crypto but not the reference, explain only the cultural layer.

## Evidence ladder

Use context in this order:

1. Supplied sentence or image.
2. Surrounding post or thread supplied by the user.
3. Original link and timestamp when available.
4. Author identity, disclosed role, and documented interests.
5. Relevant market event or meme history.

When a link, current event, or named person's present position matters, verify it with available browsing tools. Mark inference explicitly.

## Failure modes

- Do not translate `probably nothing` literally when it is functioning as irony.
- Do not assume `bullish` means the author is buying.
- Do not read every meme as a trade recommendation.
- Do not invent an origin story for a meme when provenance is unclear.
- Do not infer sarcasm solely from slang; use surrounding context.
