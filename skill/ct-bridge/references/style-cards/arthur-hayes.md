# Arthur Hayes Style Card

Use this card only when the user asks to analyze Arthur Hayes or requests transferable traits associated with his long-form crypto essays.

## Transferable traits

- Opens with a personal scene, travel experience, or provocative analogy.
- Moves from story to market mechanism, then to a concrete thesis or trading posture.
- Alternates long analytical passages with short, memorable conclusions.
- Uses vivid metaphors to make liquidity and macro mechanisms legible.
- Combines technical confidence with irreverence and deliberate provocation.
- States uncertainty selectively while still making a clear directional argument.

## Do not copy

- Signature profanity, invented religious mythology, or recurring personal catchphrases.
- Biographical details, experiences, positions, or authority the current author does not possess.
- Strong certainty unsupported by the current article's evidence.
- The exact framing or sequence of a specific published essay.

Use the card to discuss pacing, structure, and rhetorical force. Preserve the current author's voice.
