# Crypto Context Registry Starter

These entries show how to reason about register and context. They are not mandatory substitutions.

## Chinese to English

| Concept | Formal or neutral | CT native | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 接盘 | absorb the selling supply; become the final buyer | become exit liquidity; hold the bag | `exit liquidity` emphasizes the early-seller/late-buyer relationship; `holding the bag` emphasizes the eventual loss. |
| 被套后改成长持 | turn a failed short-term trade into a long-term holding | rename a bad trade “long-term conviction” | The CT form is sharper and judgmental. |
| 给持仓说话 | argue from the perspective of one's position | talk one's own book | Not identical to deliberate deception. |
| 吹自己的币 | promote one's holdings | shill one's bags | Aggressive and informal. |
| 韭菜 | retail investors repeatedly exploited by the market | exit liquidity; repeatedly harvested retail | Preserve as `the Chinese “chives” metaphor` when its cultural identity matters. |
| 流量 | audience; reach; attention | reach; engagement | Avoid `traffic` for a person's social influence. |
| 梭哈 | invest everything; take maximum exposure | go all in; full port | `full port` is strongly CT-native. |
| 追涨 | chase a rally | chase the pump | The CT form can imply poor judgment. |
| 割肉 | realize a loss; cut a losing position | cut the loss | Do not translate literally as cutting flesh. |
| 喊单 | publish a trade call | call a coin; post a call | Distinguish from regulated investment advice. |

## English to Chinese

| Phrase | Practical meaning | Context warning |
|---|---|---|
| probably nothing | 常用于反讽，暗示事情可能重要 | Sometimes repeated as a stale meme without a strong claim. |
| cooked | 状态很差、可能没救了 | Can describe a person, trade, token, or thesis. |
| we are so back | 情绪从悲观快速转向乐观 | Often exaggerated or ironic. |
| it's over | 认为行情、项目或叙事结束了 | Frequently paired with `we are so back` as a mood-cycle meme. |
| few | “懂的人很少”的省略表达 | Can signal confidence, exclusivity, or parody. |
| talking your book | 观点受到自身持仓影响 | Does not automatically mean the view is false. |
| round-trip a trade | 浮盈全部回吐，回到起点附近 | May refer to price or the trader's PnL. |
| midcurve | 嘲讽过度分析、错过简单结论的人 | Identity insult, not a technical category. |
| touch grass | 离开网络、回到现实 | Dismissive internet slang, not crypto-specific. |
| cope | 用解释安慰自己或回避失败 | Tone ranges from joking to hostile. |
