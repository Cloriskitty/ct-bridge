# Context cards

Use a context card when a direct translation would leave the reader missing the joke, stance, or social meaning.

## Compact form

```text
一句话意思
...

语气与潜台词
...

背景
...

需要注意
...
```

Omit any section that does not improve understanding.

## Evidence order

1. The supplied sentence or image
2. Surrounding posts supplied by the user
3. Original link and timestamp, when available
4. The author's disclosed role and documented interests
5. Relevant market events or meme history

Do not upgrade inference into fact merely because it fits a familiar CT narrative.

## Example

Source: `ETH is cooked. Still, probably nothing.`

- `cooked` 表示处境很糟或“没救了”，不是字面上的“煮熟”。
- `probably nothing` 在 CT 中经常是反讽，暗示某件事可能值得注意。
- 两句放在一起可能是玩梗、钓互动或故意制造矛盾感，并不能证明作者真实持仓或交易方向。
- Confidence: Medium without the surrounding thread.
